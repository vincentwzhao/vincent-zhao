VIA IMPERII

A Roman Empire Adventure

The year is 117 A.D. The Empire stretches from the mists of Britannia to the deserts beyond the Nile. You set out from Rome with little but your wits, a purse of denarii, and a season's worth of time before you must answer for how you spent it. Travel the provinces, strike deals, dodge bandits, win favor — and return to the Forum with a name worth remembering.
